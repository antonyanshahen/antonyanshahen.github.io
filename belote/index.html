Belote GS

Score pad and Bazar Blot for iPhone.

About

A score pad for Armenian Belote (Bazar Blot) and a game against the computer. Enter each round in a few taps and let the app do the counting, including Contra, Sur and kapuyt, or play Bazar Blot against the computer on three levels.

Common questions

Where are my games from the old version?

They are copied into the new version the first time you open it. Find them under History.

How do I remove ads?

Settings → Ads → Remove Ads. It is a one-time purchase and also makes hints free. On a new iPhone, use Restore purchases.

Can I play by different rules?

Settings lets you choose the winning score, whether no-trump counts double, and whether a reverse kapuyt ends the game.

How do I change the language?

The app follows your iPhone's language. Settings → Language opens the iPhone settings for the app.
