Common questions

How do my seeds grow?

Place a block over a seed, then clear that block's row or column. Each clear waters the seed once. Common seeds bloom after 3 waterings and rare seeds, with the gold ring, after 4.

What are flowers for?

Every flower you grow is yours to spend in My Garden, on plants and decorations. Some items also need a number of stars to unlock.

I bought Remove Ads on another device. How do I get it back?

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases, using the same Apple Account. Coins and other items that get used up can't be restored.

Will I keep my progress on a new phone?

Progress is saved on your device, and it is removed if you delete the app. It does not currently move between devices by itself.

Why don't I see my score on the leaderboard?

Check that you are signed in to Game Center in your device's Settings. Zen Garden scores are sent when a run ends.

How do I change my privacy choices?

You can change tracking in your device's Settings, under Privacy & Security > Tracking. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, you can also use Settings > Privacy Choices in the game.
